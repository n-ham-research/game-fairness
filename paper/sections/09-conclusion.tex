\section{Research questions}
The assignment model now has a necessary and sufficient opportunity
criterion, and hidden-opponent commitment tests have an exact recovery
criterion. They answer different questions. Further work should examine
endogenous opponent behavior and heterogeneous participant preferences,
and determine which experimentally justified restrictions retain useful
identification. External proof review and targeted novelty searches remain
necessary before publication claims.

The historical programme still requires a verified first-edition VNM
comparison. The primary-source notes distinguish Nash's bargaining input,
Shapley's coalition-value input and VNM's strategic coalition construction.
Equivariance of a solution rule preserves stabilizers of its input; this
fact alone does not identify a universal fairness standard.
