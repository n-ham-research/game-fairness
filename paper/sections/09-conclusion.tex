\section{Research questions}
The next priority is a justified assignment model with explicit global
preferences, admissible transports, timing and information, followed by
necessary and sufficient conditions for its role comparisons. A second priority
is a systematic comparison of ordered-belief tests, exchangeable-belief tests
and ex post anonymity. Existing counterexamples rule out several automatic
identifications, while leaving meaningful stronger characterizations open.

The historical programme requires a verified first-edition VNM comparison and
careful maps between strategic games, coalition opportunities and allocation
rules. The detailed literature notes preserve Nash, Shapley, Kalai--Smorodinsky,
fair division and mechanism-design comparisons with their actual domains and
source-access limits. Neither historical vocabulary nor group-theoretic
interchangeability removes the need to specify what a fairness judgment evaluates.
