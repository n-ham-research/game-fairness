\section{Research questions}
Observable role indifference identifies a specified opportunity set; richer
commitment observations identify payoff data only to the resolution of their
channels. Shared physical intervention wiring then makes simultaneous game
symmetry testable. These three distinctions organize the relationship without
defining fairness as a group property. Common monetary calibration,
within-recipient preference equivalence and robustness over common risk
attitudes answer different questions.

The main next steps are external proof criticism, targeted priority assessment
of invariant observation and deterministic-mixture identification, and better
behavioral justification of response and intervention models. Separate research gives an all-subset masking threshold and an
elementary optimal five-context design for binary five-opponent triple masks.
These design extensions remain outside this manuscript pending further scrutiny. The historical programme still requires the 1944 VNM source.
Equivariance of bargaining or coalition rules preserves input stabilizers;
it supplies neither a universal welfare calibration nor coalition stability.
