## 5. Equal opportunity versus global symmetry

A twelve-role cubic coordination game has identical complete focal tables,
full-support essential actions and trivial player image at exact/cardinal/
ordinal levels. A short analytic color certificate proves rigidity, separately
checked by exhaustive adjacency bijections. Anonymous unfolded views agree at
all depths; actual graph balls retain missing shared identities. In signedK4,
cycle parity creates two player orbits despite unsigned vertex transitivity.
The obstruction is concrete and constructive, not compatibility restated as an
automorphism definition. Its close graph-theoretic prior is explicitly credited.

## 6. Player-assignment indifference

At fixed preferences/beliefs, blind lottery indifference requires both equal
optimized role values and a common maximizing physical commitment. Universal
blind indifference recovers ALIGNED state-conditioned laws if each commitment
is exposed in at least one role somewhere. Post-role universal common-risk
indifference instead recovers menus up to action bijections under essentiality.
Common-risk versus state-dependent utilities differ; all-common-risk tests can
miss a physical lottery. Calibration and fixed action encodings are hypotheses,
not consequences of ordinal/cardinal normal-form equivalence.
