# Historical fairness: verified sources and remaining gaps

Detailed canonical source annotations are fairness-literature-vnm-strategic.md
and fairness-literature-solutions.md. They record the actual inspected edition,
draft, reprint or preview. Migration does not constitute new source inspection.

VNM: the inspected 1953 third edition (printing page1955), §28 pp255–260,
defines fairness by coalition-size dependence of the zero-sum characteristic
function; set-transitivity is sufficient. It explicitly does not guarantee
equal individual fates. Coalition laws allow joint coordination within each
side. The1944 first edition remains unread; archive metadata is not edition evidence.

Nash1950 and Kalai–Smorodinsky1975 concern bargaining rules with disagreement
points and utility-representation axioms. Shapley original content was inspected
in an identified1988 reprint, not a new1953 printing. Varian's1973 working paper,
Bogomolnaia–Moulin2001, Kohlberg–Neyman2021 and Dimitrov–Sun2025 have distinct
allocation, transfer and comparison domains; preserve the detailed notes.

The Ham2011 thesis,2011 Wikipedia revisions, Vester and Plan record is retained
in inherited historical/literature files. Do not attribute anonymous edits
beyond verified evidence. Original Dasgupta–Maskin proof bodies and the final
Plan article remain unavailable in the inspected record. No original-proof
verdict follows from access failure or a later draft.
