# Assignment models: canonical entry point

Maintain the full established arguments in assignment-and-solution-rules.md
(A1–A7) and phase4-assignment-model.md (A4.1–A4.4), with their preserved origin
and qualifications. This index avoids an independently editable duplicate.

The calibrated model fixes one participant's monetary scale, disclosed physical
transports, a reference role with t0=id, transported opponent behavior and
post-assignment action choice. Exact individual transports imply equal
conditional action values. The universal all-action/all-product-belief converse
is focal only; full game automorphisms require simultaneous tests for all recipients.

Next characterization must justify those primitives independently. A section
characterizes typed path independence, not one-shot utility indifference or
an established welfare benefit. Conditional role preference data do not fix
cross-role preferences: retain A1 throughout.
