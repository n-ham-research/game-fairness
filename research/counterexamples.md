# Counterexample index

These are migrated exact examples, not newly discovered results of this repository.

- U4: cardinal transitivity need not imply exact transitivity; ordinal
  transitivity need not imply cardinal transitivity (three binary players).
- U5: the ordinal bare swap of matrices [[0,3],[2,1]], [[0,4],[9,1]] fails
  mixed-NE preservation: (2/3,1/2) maps to a nonequilibrium.
- A1: independent role offsets preserve conditional preferences but set
  arbitrary expected role values; they need not preserve global assignment preferences.
- F2: separately identical focal opportunity tables can have incompatible
  opponent identifications and a trivial simultaneous automorphism image.
- F2: payoff menus {1,0} and {1,-1} have the same optimum but differ.
- F3: cyclic u_i=s_i+s_(i+1)-s_(i+2) has identical histogram averages but
  retains ordered-opponent effects.
- A3: uniform assignment equalizes people's prospects despite unequal roles.
- A5: invariant anti-coordination equilibrium lottery differs from its product marginals.
- VNM: zero-sum fixed payments change coalition-size fairness without changing
  singleton-action conditional preferences.
- A4.3: companion nonsplit examples have individually available exact transports
  and one-shot indifference but no coherent typed realization; repeated-lift
  discrepancies remain payoff-invisible in the base game.
