# Prioritized open questions

1. Which independently justified cross-role preference/calibration models
   admit necessary and sufficient structural opportunity conditions?
2. Which ordered versus unordered belief tests characterize focal anonymity,
   and what survives physically admissible strategy relabeling?
3. What bridges connect an exact strategic symmetry, a specified coalition or
   allocation construction, and a procedural rule's outcome requirements?
4. What changes when actions precede assignment, when opponents adapt to it,
   or when participants differ in their global preferences?
5. Can comparison criteria avoid unjustified interpersonal scales while
   retaining informative necessary and sufficient conditions?
6. Which internally proved comparison results extend known utility,
   assignment or fair-division results rather than rephrase them?

No question is answered merely by labeling an automorphism condition fair.
Choose one model before expanding the general programme.
