# Literature review and novelty limits

Read fairness-literature-solutions.md and fairness-literature-vnm-strategic.md
for source-specific equations, page ranges, domains and actual access limits.
Ham2011 already treats ordinal/cardinal game isomorphisms and equilibrium
preservation; utility-invariance.md explicitly attributes that prior work.

The manuscript uses no universal normative fairness theorem. Canonical
normalizations, finite-range kernel observations, calibrated assignment tests
and elementary averaging are proved, with novelty unclaimed or unresolved.
The candidate binary-potential normalization consequences depend on the
Game Symmetry theorem and its separate targeted novelty comparison.

Priority source tasks: verified1944VNM body; finalPlan; originalDM proofs;
a precise comparison with cardinal-utility/assignment literature for any
proposed characterization. Do not repeat failed access searches absent a
new lead, and do not infer novelty from missing terminology or failed searches.
