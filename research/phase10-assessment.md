## Literature comparison actually supported

Close prior is strongest for the graph branch: signed-graph switching and its
automorphism projection already have explicit theorems (Zaslavsky), and regular
universal covers/graph realization are established. The construction theorem
used is version-qualified because the 2025 source corrects an older construction.
No new cohomological theorem or group-extension classification is claimed.

Polynomial identity, support separation, minimax and value-of-information
inequalities are standard. Kreps menu preference is conceptually close, but
only the primary author abstract was inspected; its full proof and subsequent
finite menu-identification literature are still a priority gap. Blackwell's
all-decision-problem comparison is richer than our fixed menus and restricted
utility models. The risk counterexample directly proves why those quantifiers
cannot be silently equated. Epstein–Schneider's 2002 primary working text was
inspected for rectangularity; this phase does not reprove its axiomatization.
Earlier exact sources on Nash, Shapley, Varian, random assignment and VNM remain
version-qualified in the canonical literature notes. No first-edition1944 body,
external specialist opinion, or priority clearance has been invented.

## Strongest next questions

1. Find a substantively justified game class with positive gluing from local
   opportunities to global interchangeability BEYOND graph switching. A natural
   class restriction must do work: adding 'the maps are compatible' is not a
   theorem. Compare minimal joint/coalitional opportunity data and identify
   precisely which interactions they preserve. Do not turn this into mask design.
2. Investigate universal assignment comparisons with endogenous opponent behavior
   justified by their incentives, rather than an arbitrary fixed or worst-case
   response. Preserve belief, risk and physical commitment timing explicitly.
3. Audit the decision/menu literature for A2/R2/R3 and seek a result that remains
   meaningful when decision-essentiality fails, without simply adding every
   missing action as an experimental singleton menu. Characterize a natural
   behavioral quotient, rather than recover irrelevant labels.
4. Explore preference models treating role identity itself as a consequence.
   Determine what impartiality axioms justify a global calibration; do not
   infer it from independent within-role utility representations or choose
   canonical ranks as welfare by convenience.

## Publication recommendation

No current result is demonstrated to be the basis of an important ORIGINAL
paper. The strongest coherent direction is calibrated assignment fairness
with explicit timing and the exact limit of its structural implications.
The graph/switching work is valuable supporting impossibility evidence; it
cannot carry a major novelty claim because close prior is explicit. A2/R2/R3
are the best provisional theorem candidates for a focused literature audit
and further mathematical strengthening, not a submission recommendation.

Keep the eleven-page manuscript unchanged this phase; its observation-heavy
narrative should not dictate foundational research. Do not write another
manuscript to collect elementary propositions. Seek external specialist
criticism only after separate authorization; none is contacted here. The
canonical Game Symmetry and Agent Games repositories remain read-only.
