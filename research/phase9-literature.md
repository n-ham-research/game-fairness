# Phase 9 source comparisons and novelty limits

16 October 2026. Source comparison is not originality clearance.

The new rank/factorization tests are standard finite linear estimability:
only the discrepancy subspace needs recovery. Restricted minimum singular
values, least-squares inverse error, error-ball separation and bounded-sample
concentration are established inverse-problem/statistical tools. Applying
them to calibrated game transports does not create a new general theory of
linear measurements. A claim to a novel fairness contribution would require
an independently motivated role/intervention model and a comparison with
statistical symmetry testing, not just a new label for a kernel inclusion.

Hoeffding's original article was located at the publisher:
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830
(*Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random Variables*, JASA58,
1963,13–30). The publisher abstract confirms scope. An institutional original
scan https://www.cs.rpi.edu/academics/courses/spring06/random/hoefding.pdf
opened as an image-only PDF; no readable theorem body was obtained through
this pass's tools, and the local download failed DNS. No proof-body reading
is claimed. The new research note supplies the complete standard exponential-
tilt proof of the precise concentration bound it uses, including its constant.

A targeted primary tensor source was actually inspected: Andrew Schaffer
Berget, *Symmetries of Tensors*, University of Minnesota PhD dissertation,
August2009, institutional author text:
https://conservancy.umn.edu/bitstreams/e9859b01-8423-40ed-ac25-8b1d8b3ffa2a/download .
Printed pp.18 and81–85 describe row/column/Young symmetrizers, their generated
ideals and the Schur–Weyl decomposition. These are established algebraic
objects governing our uniform subset averages. The thesis studies
representations generated by decomposable tensors and matroid restrictions;
we have not found or excluded an equivalent minimum-mask design theorem
there. This is partial source inspection, not a full107-page audit.

P8-O4's rectangle threshold is a direct application of standard decomposition
and branching, now independently reconstructed again. P9-M2 replaces that
machinery in one finite setting by Boolean monomials and unsigned incidence
ranks. Those tools are standard; the exact five-context graph formulation
remains a candidate application with no priority claim. Literature on
experimental design, harmonic multilinear polynomials and symmetrizer spans
must be checked before calling it new. A bounded search yielded related
tensor sources, not a specific prior theorem matching this design claim.

The utility and normative distinctions from the inspected VNM third edition,
Nash/KS originals and Shapley reprint remain in the canonical literature notes.
No first-edition VNM scan or external human review was newly obtained.
Approximate role indifference is a comparison in one participant's calibrated
utility; representation invariance of within-role preferences does not supply
that calibration. Small exact-scale errors alone do not imply equal realized
outcomes, coalition stability or morally preferred assignments.

The proposed agent feasibility report is a separate local artifact; its
source checks do not change the canonical prior-result status here. No
statistical observation context is silently treated as a one-trial signal,
a finite-bit message or a fixed-objective LLM agent.
