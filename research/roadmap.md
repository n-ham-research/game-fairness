# Game Fairness research roadmap

Initial split, 16 October 2026. This programme has equal intellectual priority
with Game Symmetry, but its publication maturity differs. Read the migration
record and theorem-ledger before research changes. The initial manuscript is a
concise foundation draft, not a completed fairness characterization.

1. Choose one independently motivated participant-assignment model. Specify
   global cross-role preferences, physical consequences and admissible strategy
   correspondences, timing, information and opponent behavior.
2. Derive necessary and sufficient assignment-comparison conditions; challenge
   them with the existing calibration, focal-versus-simultaneous and optimized-
   versus-menu counterexamples before adding stronger symmetry hypotheses.
3. Classify ordered, exchangeable and IID belief tests. Distinguish ex post
   anonymity from information loss and identify which richer tests recover it.
4. Connect structural opportunities, procedural equivariance and outcome
   criteria through explicit maps; preserve counterexamples to blanket arrows.
5. Compare actual primary sources. First-edition VNM, final Plan and original
   Dasgupta–Maskin proof bodies remain source gaps, not assumed confirmations.
6. Independently review significant proposed theorems, investigate novelty and
   obtain external mathematical criticism before publication decisions.

The binary exact-potential theorem remains in Game Symmetry. Its cardinal and
ordinal corollaries are recorded here with a companion dependency. The
six-player nonsplitting construction is not this programme's centrepiece.
No manuscript submission is authorized.

Phase 7 completed a calibrated choice-after-assignment model and a separate
commitment information model. Next: external proof criticism, objective-state
menu/Blackwell novelty comparisons, endogenous opponent behavior, and the
1944 VNM source gap. See phase7-literature-and-status.md.

Phase 8: see phase8-report.md. Prioritize game-specific separating designs,
stable identification, strategically justified response/selection models and
weighted deterministic-mixture novelty assessment. Keep research-only all-r
and observable-coherence extensions outside the main narrative pending scrutiny.
