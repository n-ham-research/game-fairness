# Conjectures and research hypotheses

No universal implication from utility invariance to fairness is established.
Do not promote the programme's arrows to theorems. A new conjecture must
specify the assignment consequences, participant preference domain, admissible
transports, timing and belief quantifiers, and name a potential counterexample.

Current research hypotheses are intentionally open: which justified assignment
models admit an automorphism characterization; which restricted belief tests
recover focal anonymity; and which structural conditions combine with a
procedural rule to guarantee a specified outcome criterion.
