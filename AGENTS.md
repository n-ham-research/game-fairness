# Research instructions

Author: Nicholas Ham. All games are finite unless explicitly stated.
Compose right to left; (P_p s)_j=s_{p^{-1}(j)}. Distinguish literature, prior
Ham results, corrections, proved research candidates, conjectures and open
questions. Novelty remains provisional; internal AI agreement is not external
peer review. Preserve source versions, exact arithmetic, counterexamples,
rejected conjectures and accurate actual AI contribution records.

For major claims use separate proof, counterexample and strengthening reviews
where practical. Read research/roadmap.md, research/theorem-ledger.md and
reviews/review-log.md before mathematical edits. Do not submit to arXiv or a
journal without explicit user authorization. Do not redistribute third-party
PDFs. Keep research history and commit provenance. Run scripts/build.sh and
python3 -m unittest discover -s experiments before pushing; inspect rendered
pages after layout edits. Use the native LaTeX editor when available.

## Project boundary

This repository is game-fairness, the canonical home for utility invariance,
player-assignment models, opponent ordering and fairness foundations. Do not
modify ../game-symmetry without explicit authorization. Cite companion result
IDs and exact commits through research/cross-project-dependencies.md. Report
suspected errors in research/companion-notices.md, rather than silently fixing
the other project. Structural imports and research/origins are frozen snapshots.
Do not restart migrated research or present copied results as independently
obtained. Do not define fairness to be symmetry to manufacture equivalence.
Distinguish structural, procedural and outcome fairness; ordinal maps preserve
pure equilibria but need not preserve mixed equilibria. No unjustified
interpersonal utility comparisons. Binary exact-potential research belongs to
the companion symmetry programme.
